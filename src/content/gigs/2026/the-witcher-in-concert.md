Starting with [*The Trail*](https://music.apple.com/gb/album/the-trail/1333501415?i=1333501416), we were musically accompanied through Geralt and Ciri's journey winding across the desolate marshlands of Velen, the earthly excesses of Novigrad and Oxenfurt, and the snow-capped isles of Skellige. We fell through portals, fought spectres and beasts, defeated the Wild Hunt, and relived one of the most endearing father-daughter stories ever told in the medium of gaming. It didn't end there; we went on to solve Master Mirror's riddle and drank sweet wine from Toussaint.

Appreciating the music while playing the game is one thing, but listening to a full ensemble of musicians, some of whom composed said music, is a real treat. I hope they tour again next year. I will be first in line, no storm can stop me.
