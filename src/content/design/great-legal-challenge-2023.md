I got to know the team at The Turnaround Project through my wife. She used to volunteer with them while she was reading for her Master's degree at [Queen's University](https://www.qub.ac.uk/). She had suggested that I could help them by designing a poster for this event (free of charge, of course), and I was glad to help.
