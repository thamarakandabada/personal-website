Martin McDonagh's films have just the right amounts of whimsy, sadness, absurdity, and profundity for me. I am a fan. I am also a huge admirer of both Colin Farell and Brendan Gleeson, the two leads in this film. This is their second project with McDonagh, after the excellent 2008 outing *In Bruges*, and it did not disappoint.
