I always say that my favourite concert film is *The Last Waltz* and that *Stop Making Sense* is a close second. I have designed a poster for the latter, but not for the former; make of that what you will.

Ever since I purchased the A24 4K Blu-ray release, I have found myself rewatching this film many times, and it never looses its electric charm. It's bursting with energy from start to finish; a quality I haven't experienced in any other concert film I've seen.
