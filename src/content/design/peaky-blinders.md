This is the first TV poster I designed. I spent too much time trying to tweak the colour palette, and the end result was a strange combination of orange and green which does not look good at all, now that I look back at it.

Along with the rest of the world, I was a fan of the show. Tommy Shelby is truly one of the best TV characters ever written, and this performance cemented Cillian Murphy as one of the greats in my mind.
