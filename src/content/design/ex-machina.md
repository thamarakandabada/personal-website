When designing film posters, I like to look for memorable scenes or moments I can feature on them. This dance scene from Ex Machina is one such moment. It's not the sort of thing you would see on an official poster, but that's not my focus. Since these are essentially fan-made creations with no actual link to the filmmakers or the studios themselves, I have complete freedom to design them any way I like.
