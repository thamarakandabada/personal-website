Goodfellas is an eminently rewatchable classic. The journey we go on with Henry Hill is visceral and unenviable. The "oner" is perhaps the best known moment of this film, but my favourite bit is the montage with the *Layla* outro playing over it.

I bonded with a co-worker over our shared love of this film, and when he retired, I gifted him a framed copy of this poster. He loved it.
