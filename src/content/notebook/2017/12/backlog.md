I’ve been busy with work for the most part, but I took a little break in Vietnam, too. Everything somehow added up to me not writing even when I wanted to.

Writing was not the only thing I didn’t do. I couldn't reach my 40-books-a-year goal, too. I’ve currently hit the brakes at 32, which I think where I will still be when the year comes to an end in 5 more days.

There’s much to talk about. For starters, many exciting things have happened in my startup life. I also want to share some habits that didn't quite work out for me. Then I want to reflect on this past year, which I have very good reason to believe was the best year of my life so far.

I will see you when I do.
