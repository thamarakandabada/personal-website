My journaling habit never really took off, but it has produced some indecisive and random strings of thought that found their way to the Cloud. Having chanced upon them this morning I found myself craving to go back to journaling again. Whether I would is a question for tomorrow’s dawn to answer. Until then, enjoy this sneak peek to my [Monkey Mind](https://www.pocketmindfulness.com/understanding-monkey-mind-live-harmony-mental-companion/) that follows.

This is uncensored me (save for some confidential business information I’ve taken the liberty to redact), purely for comic relief. Give yourself a good chuckle.

## July 13

Since I didn’t write in the mornings since the 9th, here goes.

The days have been crazy. Not because I’ve been working, although I would’ve like to say so. I was sick for 2 days, probably due to an allergy I developed because of some shit I ate. Those were basically 2 days off work.

Since I’m not getting those back now I have to make the most out of whatever is coming up. The past 2 days have been productive so far. I need to keep riding this wave.

The socks are here and we’re putting them up on the website today, but the underwear are nowhere near the beginning yet. We haven’t found a good supplier who can do the full garment at a reasonable price.

We’re also in the process of making some design changes to the website.  
So I don’t want to make this a long post since I honestly can’t think of anything more to write.

## July 15

Late wakeup today.

And my body feels strangely beat up. When I went to sleep yesterday I was a little uncomfortable, with a neck pain for the most part. Perhaps this is it manifesting.

Yesterday we made some design changes to the website and launched socks. One person has bought socks so far.

One of the interesting things about the business is how slow it is. This is because we still haven’t broken through that friends circle. Almost all the people who’ve bought stuff from us are friends, or friends of friends. This surely won’t sustain the business.

Facebook ads will be a good way for people who don’t know us yet to discover us. But I have this stubborn rule against using advertising until we’ve grown organically up to the point we no longer can. And we surely can grow a little more.

## July 19

Here it comes again, I can’t think of anything to write. I guess the only way to get over this is to keep on writing every day.

## September 30 (Today)

Coming back to the journal after more than one-and-a-half-months, I hardly have anything to say.

A lot of things have happened and I’ve completely redone my calendar now. I plan to start sticking to it fully from tomorrow morning (yup.) Until then, in typical irresponsible procrastinator fashion I’m going to cut myself some slack.

A couple of weeks ago I decided to write my startup story on Medium. The idea was to write one post a week or so, each time discussing a different aspect of the business: setting up, customers, suppliers, etc. I did that for exactly two weeks until the procrastination gene overpowered me.

The excuse was always the same, “I don’t have time to write this, I’d rather be working on something else,” after which I’d always go back to doing something horrifyingly unproductive.

Tomorrow, things will change.
