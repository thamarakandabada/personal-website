This is a full excerpt from an entry I made in my journal this morning. (I use [Day One](https://dayoneapp.com), if you’re curious.)

<blockquote>I’m starting a new journal today. It will serve one purpose, and one purpose only.<br><br>Every time I feel like reaching for my phone and browsing mindlessly through social media feeds, I will try and come to this journal to write something instead. That way, I will be doing something I’ve always wanted to do more of (i.e., write) while keeping my mind away from useless drivel.<br><br>I will have no plans or tactics or goals for this journal like I had for the previous one. I will write whatever I feel like writing about at the moment.</blockquote>

I named it my _Distraction Journal_. Not very creative, I know, but not pretentious either.

I wrote this at the and of the entry:

<blockquote>Maybe I will look back on this someday and write a blog post about it.</blockquote>

Maybe I will.
