I happened upon the [This American Life](https://www.thisamericanlife.org/) website the other day, and fell absolutely and utterly in love with the typeface. After a quick search I found out that it was called [Maiola](https://www.type-together.com/maiola-font), designed by [Veronika Burian](https://www.type-together.com/veronika-burian) of [TypeTogether](https://www.type-together.com/) (Futura, Athelas). I _had to_ get it.

To my great delight, I discovered that the font was available through Adobe Fonts. It was easy to load it up with [Fonts Plugin](https://fontsplugin.com/) and replace Big Caslon FB, my headings font. Rig Sans stays intact as the base font, I think that and Maiola make a great pairing.

Have a browse, and let me know what you think!
