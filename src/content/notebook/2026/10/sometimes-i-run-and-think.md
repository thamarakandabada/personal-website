It's getting colder here. That makes running outside slightly less appealing. Nuwani and I had been talking about joining a gym for the winter so we could keep up our weekly running habit, and today, we did.

We didn't want to sink a lot of money into this. Really, all we needed was access to a treadmill and shelter from the elements. [PureGym](https://www.puregym.com/) in the UK operates affordable 24x7 gyms and it seemed like the perfect option for us. We decided to try the gym in the Belfast city centre. It costs £22.99 per month—no frills, just access.

Understandably, it was packed today when we walked in. It was around 6pm; the offices in the city had just emptied. Our master plan to make use of an easily accessible and cheap gym, it turned out, was hardly genius; a lot of people clearly had the same idea. We were relieved, however, to find that many treadmills were free.

Treadmills make me a bit uneasy. They don't feel as natural as running on terra firma. It always takes me a few minutes to adjust to the very slightly askew gait, and even after speeding up to a comfortable pace, I cannot stop thinking about the ground running away from me, and me having to catch up—it's an odd sensation when you're used to leaping across solid ground. To add to the awkwardness, my hand kept hitting the unfortunately placed emergency stop button. All in all, it wasn't as smooth a run as I had hoped it would be.

But we're committed to doing this now for at least a few months. Who knows, I may even be tempted to try some of the other machines. 

We're going back on Thursday. And then again on Sunday. And onwards, three days a week.
