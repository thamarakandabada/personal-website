The other day I received James' zine titled [*A Creative's Guide to Personal Websites*](https://jamesg.blog/2026/09/05/creative-web-guide-zine) in the post. It combines a lot of topics he has been writing about for a while: the importance of the personal web, why have a website, being creative on the web and making it fun, and ideas for making a personal website of your own. It's a quick read, and lays out the basic ideas in a concise and easily digestible manner. 

In an era when the web is crawling (pun intended) with bots and is seemingly abundant with machine-created slop, having a personal website may seem futile. But I think it's more important than ever to repopulate the web with human creativity. We need more quirky personal corners on the web, built not just by developers and techies, but creatives, too. Resources like this help. You can find PDF and text versions of the zine on James' website linked above. Send it to a creative person in your life. Tell them they should bring their gifts to the open web.

For those with no technical background, making a website may seem like a daunting task. It really isn't. You can find more resources on my [IndieWeb](/indieweb) page. If you need any ideas, help, or inspiration, please feel free to [contact me](/hello) any time.
