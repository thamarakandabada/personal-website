Belfast's North Street is not a fancy neighbourhood. Years of neglect and rising costs have caused independent businesses to move out, leaving derelict buildings in their wake. The controversial [Tribeca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tribeca_Belfast) development has played a part. Keats & Chapman, a bookshop selling second-hand books, had endured for many years, but now it looks like they're [on the way out](https://www.irishnews.com/news/northern-ireland/death-of-belfast-bookshops-continues-with-beloved-city-centre-store-set-to-pull-shutters-down-for-good-K3YMPH45UBBVFLXVKHPUTS7MOQ/), too. They are in the process of being evicted. Relocation prospects are slim, and the move itself will be a costly task.

If we're walking past this area, we always go in. It's a veritable treasure trove, an Aladdin's Cave of sorts, of old tomes, magazines, board games, and other curios.

There is still hope, seemingly, as a GoFundMe campaign set up to save the bookshop and help them relocate has gained some traction over the last few days. Please help if you can so that these treasures can be preserved for future generations. [Here is the link](https://www.gofundme.com/f/save-our-bookshop-keats-chapman-belfast).
