This month's Homebrew Website Club Europe events have now been scheduled. You can find a list of them [here](https://events.indieweb.org/tag/gmt). The events are hosted by [James](https://jamesg.blog/), and I will be in the background helping out.

> Homebrew Website Club is a meetup for anyone interested in personal websites and a distributed web. Whether you’re a blogger, coder, designer, or just someone who wants to improve their presence on the web, this meetup is for you.

Do join us. It's a great group of people, and this is a great place for inspiration if you're thinking about putting together a personal website for the first time, or improving and expanding the site you currently have. The conversation is informal and flows freely. If you don't feel like actively participating, you can simply sit back and listen. No pressure.

Hope to see you there.
