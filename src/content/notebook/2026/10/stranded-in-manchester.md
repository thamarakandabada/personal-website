Having made my way back to the hotel after an excellent evening in Manchester immersed in the [music of The Witcher](/gigs/2026/the-witcher-in-concert) last night, I opened my email client to find a notice from the airline informing me that my return flight to Belfast has been cancelled. Not a worry, they had booked me on a later flight at no charge. I went to bed, comfortable in the knowledge that I could sleep in a bit without scrambling to get to the airport in the morning.

When I woke up today I checked my emails again. I wanted to download my boarding pass before heading to the airport. This is when it hit me; the new flight is not later today, it is tomorrow! The combination of post-show excitement and the exhaustion of having been battered by the elements all evening must have dulled my faculties; I misread the flight information.

Panic setting in, I browsed through the Manchester Airport's website to find that all local flights today have been cancelled. I had no choice; going home today wasn't an option.

I informed Nuwani, and told my colleagues I will miss work tomorrow. I had a meeting booked for the morning, which also had to be rescheduled. I talked to the hotel reception and extended my stay for another night. All that was left to do was to find some way of spending the time.

With strong winds and rain in the forecast for the rest of the day, going into the city didn't seem like a good idea. I am staying in an airport hotel about 20 minutes away from the city by train. Manchester has many things to offer, and unlike Belfast, most places stay open on a Sunday. But after last night's experience, I wasn't keen on venturing out again.

Am I going to have to stay in my hotel the whole time? I must admit that doesn't sound entirely awful. I quite like staying indoors. I could work on the website a little (there are always things to fix), or write a blog post or two (look, this one's already finished!). I could call my friends. I have a book, too. And there is a gym at the hotel next door, which I can access for free. Yes, I will head there in the evening. I won't miss my Sunday Long Run.

I don't regret the trip, because the performance last night was incredible. In a few hours I will be home again.
