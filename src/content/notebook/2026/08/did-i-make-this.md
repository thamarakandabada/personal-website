I have used Lovable (a Claude wrapper) in the past to make some tools for personal use. My [physical media vault](https://physicalmedia.thamara.co.uk/), [financial data visualiser](https://finviz.thamara.co.uk/), and a few other apps hosted on a personal subdomain were made with it. "Vibe-coding" is the term that generally describes this practice, and I used it to refer to my projects when I [blogged about them](/notebook/topics/vibe-coding/). Recently I have developed a slight aversion to it. I have not become completely unsympathetic to the term, but I have grown a bit wary of it, just like I would have with most other buzzwords in the technology space.

Similarly, over the last few months alone, my thoughts on AI-based tools have changed drastically<sup><a href="#1">1</a></sup> as I read and learn more about them. The initial enthusiasm I had when tools like ChatGPT and Claude first became widely available to the public has waned somewhat. I've made a conscious effort to limit my use of AI in personal life (although I don't always have that luxury at work).<sup><a href="#2">2</a></sup>

I am happy with the tools I made, and have been using them regularly. I put a lot of thought into them; the design, the typography, all the functionality I wanted them to have, were all carefully chosen by me. But I didn't write a single line of code. Can I still say that I made them?

A casual online conversation that happened the other day gave me cause to think about this. [Tabitha](https://tabitha.computer/) mentioned that she had been browsing my physical media collection app. I immediately said that I didn't make it myself, and then [James](https://jamesg.blog/) said that it still had my "essence" (or some words to that effect, I can't remember exactly). I added that I would take full credit for how the app looked and worked, because I prompted Lovable to make it that way.

Can something I didn't create have my _essence_? Am I ascribing too narrow a definition to the word _create_ here? Was the coding tool in this instance simply a vehicle for my creativity, or something else entirely? Where does the _essence_ of billions of lines of code Claude was trained on to develop the ability to spit out any functionality I wanted within a few seconds end, and I begin?

This is not an attempt to arrive at an answer, or even to argue the fact one way or the other. I may be inclined to engage in that exercise some day, but for now I simply want to put this in writing, as a record of sorts. To plant a flag. A reminder to my future self that I have been thinking about this.
