More times than I'd care to count, I've tried to start a journaling habit. I've tried different [methods](/notebook/topics/journaling) to make it stick. It never did.

I've bought various notebooks over the years and tried taking notes at work and in general in life. I would always give up after overdoing it for a few days.

At one point in my life I started organising my days meticulously in my calendar. I had alloted time slots for sleep, meals, social media, hobbies—it was bordering on ridiculous. That lasted a few short months.

In the height of the pandemic, I played Wordle every day, until I discovered a [hack](/notebook/2022/01/a-wordle-hack) and it became boring. I could have ignored the hack and kept on playing, but I didn't.

When I first bought a PS4, I developed a habit of obsessively playing games to completion. I accrued [a few Platinum trophies](https://psnprofiles.com/thamarakandabada/log?type=platinum) thanks to this. It became a chore after a while and I simply stopped.

A few years ago I started playing the [Mini Crossword](https://www.nytimes.com/crosswords/game/mini) on the New York Times every day. It evolved into a habit of playing a number of their word games, including [Connections](https://www.nytimes.com/games/connections), [Spelling Bee](https://www.nytimes.com/puzzles/spelling-bee), and [Strands](https://www.nytimes.com/games/strands). It fizzled out after a while.

When LinkedIn first introduced word games, I played them every day. I had a close-to-400-day streak, until one day I missed it, and never picked it up again.

I have tried on various things over the years; not many of them have stuck. Various exercise routines, meal plans, writing challanges, [meditation](/notebook/topics/meditation/)—I've tried it all. There are *some* things I've managed to continue doing, for example, my [running](/notebook/topics/running/), and this August blogging streak. I also listen to podcasts every day, a habit I've maintained for more than 10 years.

Seemingly disparate interests. Unrelated habits.

What does this say about me?
