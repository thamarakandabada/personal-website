When I first heard about [Blaugust](https://nerdgirlthoughts.game.blog/2026/07/15/blaugust-2026-is-coming/), an annual blogging challenge where participants post 31 times during the month of August, I thought, "I'm never going to be able to do that". I was intrigued, however. Not enough to sign up officially as a participant, but I wanted to use this as an excuse to try and write as much as possible this month. I remember telling a friend, "I will be an unofficial participant... meaning, I will make a concerted effort to post at least 10 posts this month".

Looking back at my blog post [archive](/notebook/archives), it is clear that on occasion entire years have passed without me writing anything. So, 10 posts a month was a high bar for me. My biggest concern was coming up with topics to write about. How could I possibly have enough to fill 10 posts within a month?

20 days in, I look back at those concerns with mild amusement. So far, writing inspiration has not been an issue. If it ever became an issue, I have discovered there is a great community of people around me who can help.

It helps that I don't write anything *of substance*. They're mostly personal updates, meta posts about this website itself, and sometimes, short rants. I couldn't imagine writing long and well-researched think pieces every day. At least, not with the current responsibilities I have. What matters is that I'm exercising this muscle; the quality of the output is secondary to me at this stage.

I'm off to Manchester tomorrow. Of course, I'll take the laptop with me. I will write at the airport. I have a lot of standing around to do as well, at a gig. I will write there. I will write at the hotel. I will take notes at the Manchester Museum and the Whitworth Art Gallery I'm visiting. 

I will keep the streak active.
