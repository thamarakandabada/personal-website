I have discovered yet another writing app.

This one lets me connect my Astro project's [content collections](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/content-collections/), pulls the defined schema from my `content.config.ts` file, and lets me write Markdown in a distraction-free environment. It's not a note-taking app that I can use on the go for developing blog post ideas, like [Bear](https://bear.app/). It's a writing app built specifically for Astro.

The developer Danny Smith has a video explaining how the app works [here](https://youtu.be/SJkXD59gQ5U).

I'm writing this post on the app. I like the minimalist interface. And the fact that my frontmatter can easily be edited in-app using a CMS-like interface is a big plus.

I have now added this to my publishing workflow. I will be testing it over the next few weeks. The app is open source. You can find more information and download links [here](https://astroeditor.danny.is/).
