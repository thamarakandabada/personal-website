I ran 3.5 miles today after a short break. My last run was more than a week ago: on Sunday, 30 August. I had run 6.29 miles (just over 10K) that day; my longest run ever. It was raining, and I had been sick for a day or two—a very annoying cold, with violent coughing fits. I should not have run that day, but I did.

The result: being out of commission for more than a week. Summer is officially over in Northern Ireland, and it's raining now almost every day. Going running in the rain while battling a cold was asking for trouble, so I decided to take it easy until I felt better.

Today's run wasn't difficult, but I ran slower than normal. As I'm writing this now, my legs are starting to hurt—that's not normal and was never an issue when I was running regularly (3 times a week). This isn't putting me off at all; quite the opposite. I am realising how important it is to keep at the habit without lapsing. I am forgiving myself for taking a short break while I was sick, but I won't be so easy on me if I ever stop running out of laziness. 

Onwards.
