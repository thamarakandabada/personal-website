In March, I found myself [listening to](https://www.last.fm/user/thamarak/listening-report/month) some old favourites — The Beatles, The Smiths, RHCP, REM — and a number of artists who were completely new to me.

Out of my new discoveries, I can particularly recommend Jeff Tweedy and Dark, Dark, Dark. Do have a listen to Tweedy’s _Love Is The King_ and DDD’s _Bright Bright Bright_.

I’ve used [soundiiz.com](https://soundiiz.com/) to duplicate my Deezer playlist in Apple Music and Spotify.

Enjoy the playlists!
