We caught up with sports writer Andrew Fidel Fernando for a chat on his first book, the well received travelogue Upon a Sleepless Isle.

**Andrew Fidel Fernando** is a prolific sports writer with a penchant for incisive analysis of all things cricket. He is also a historian and travel writer, and his new book Upon A Sleepless Isle has been well received both here and abroad.

Andrew is on Twitter as [@afidelf](https://twitter.com/afidelf).

Andrew’s book is available in select local bookstores, [Takas.lk](https://web.archive.org/web/20221129154422/https://takas.lk/upon-a-sleepless-isle-by-andrew-fidel-fernando.html), and [Amazon](https://web.archive.org/web/20221129154422/https://www.amazon.com/Upon-Sleepless-Andrew-Fidel-Fernando-ebook/dp/B07SQQZ1C6).
