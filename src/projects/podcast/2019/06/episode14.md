From Doo Daruwo to Soora Pappa, from Rookantha to Bathiya & Santhush, the 1990s saw an unprecedented boom in popular entertainment in Sri Lanka that defined an entire generation. This is a look back at the glory days.

**Dilina Amaruwan Pathirage** is a writer at Roar Media. He has a BA in Teaching English as a Second Language and has extensive experience in feature writing, content creation and writing for online media. He also been involved in video production during his stint at Yamu.lk, having being featured on a few videos of his own.

Dilina is a self-confessed film buff and a pop culture enthusiast.

Dilina is on Twitter at [@dili](https://web.archive.org/web/20221129141526/https://twitter.com/Dili).

**Chav** is a programming language pony by day and everything else by night. He’s on Twitter at [@chav\_](https://web.archive.org/web/20221129141526/https://twitter.com/chav_).
