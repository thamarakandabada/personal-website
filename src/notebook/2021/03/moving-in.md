To push myself to write, I started publishing my incomplete thoughts in a section on my website sometime ago. I called it a Microblog. These were strings of thoughts and ideas that were too long for Twitter and too short for fully-fledged blog posts. I kept at it fairly regularly in the beginning.

Cut to a few months later, I had fallen out of the habit of microblogging, save for a few monthly updates about the music I was listening to, and I thought a change of scenery might help.

So, now, the microblog is here, in [a new section on my blog.](https://thamara.co.uk/section/microblog/) I’ll try to fill this space more regularly.
