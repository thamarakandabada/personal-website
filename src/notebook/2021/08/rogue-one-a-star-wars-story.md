_Rogue One_ is my 2nd favourite Star Wars movie after _Empire_. I loved it so much when it came out, I saw it three times in the theatre.

I bought the 4K Blu-ray disc a few weeks ago and was pleased to find that the 4K transfer is every bit as good as I had hoped. The movie was shot digitally in 6.5K, which means that fine details are exceptionally preserved — and the resulting image is pretty sharp. The HDR10 grading also makes bright highlights pop and the blacks deep and inky.

In terms of story, the movie speaks for itself.

**Verdict:** Highly recommended.
