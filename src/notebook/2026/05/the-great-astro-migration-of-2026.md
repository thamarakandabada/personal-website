I've been thinking of ditching WordPress for a static site generator (SSG) for some time now. WordPress has been great for the last 12 years, and I have no gripes with it as such (except the block editor, which just refuses to co-operate sometimes), but rebuilding a (version of this) site from the ground up is going to be an exciting project, and a perfect way to keep my novelty-craving brain occupied.

I've decided [Astro](https://astro.build/) is the best tool for me to use at this stage, with deployment in [Vercel](https://vercel.com/home). Both have extensive and beginner-friendly documentation.

I am in no great hurry to get this finished; it's simply going to be a side project which I can dip in and out of whenever I have time. This site will continue to be updated as normal.

Here are some of the changes/additions I'm thinking of making:

- **Typography:** Use of [Maiola](https://www.type-together.com/maiola-font) throughout, inspired by the [This American Life](https://www.thisamericanlife.org/) website.

- **Colour palette:** Minor tweaks to my [current colour palette](https://thamara.co.uk/colophon#colour-palette).

- **Archives:** Current archives such as [/gigs](/gigs/), [/desk](/desk/), and [/podroll](/podroll/) to be re-written as a file-based (`.md`) content collections.

- **Codex:** Adding a new `/codex` (or `/canon`) page, version-controlled in Git. (Inspired by [Buster Benson](https://busterbenson.com/codex)).

I've just got started. The code is open-source. Follow along [here](https://github.com/thamarakandabada/personal-website), and see blog updates [here](/notebook/topics/the-great-astro-migration-of-2026/).
