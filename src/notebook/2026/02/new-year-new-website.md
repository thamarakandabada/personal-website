It's been a while since the last iteration of my website, **thamara.xyz.** It was mostly [text-based](https://web.archive.org/web/20220926002257/https://thamara.xyz/).

I bought the **thamara.co.uk** and **thamara.uk** domains last year, and thought I should give this space a new look.

I've refreshed everything. The homepage, the blog (which has now been moved back to WordPress from Medium), and everything else. I've added a number of new pages, too:

**[Colophon](https://thamara.co.uk/colophon/):** read all about how and why the website was made here.

**[Now](https://thamara.co.uk/now/):** Inspired by Derek Sivers's [nownownow.com](http://nownownow.com). Read all about what's happening in my life right now on this page. I've added a number of widgets to this page by vibe-coding them. Make of that what you will. It worked for me, and I think the purpose is innocent enough.

**[Uses](https://thamara.co.uk/uses/):** Inspired by Wes Bos's [uses.tech](http://uses.tech). I've listed the day to day tools I use (both hardware and software) on this page.

The thinking behind the /now and /uses pages is simple. They're a window into my life, although I cannot think of a single reason why anybody would be interested (my own wife was unimpressed when I showed her.) I don't know why I still have a website at all; perhaps it's out of boredom more than anything else. I like having things to tinker with.

I've also created an [RSS](https://thamara.co.uk/notebook/feed) feed for the blog. How exciting! Hello there, if you're reading this (unlikely anybody will be.)

That's it for now. Time will tell how long this iteration will last.
