Starting the day before yesterday, for no particular reason at all, I have decided to do a coffee detox. For a month. I don't know if I will last that long, but that is the plan. Yes, I'm doing okay; this is just a silly idea that had been implanted in my brain some time ago. Now I cannot rest until I try it out.

I woke up with headaches yesterday and today; I'm sure that's unrelated.

Normally, I have 3-5 cups of coffee a day. The first one is consumed right after I wake up. On weekdays, I have a second one around 10am at the office. Depending on how the day goes, I would have another one or two between 12pm and 4pm. If I'm going to the cinema after work, I always have a mocha at the bar. Coffee doesn't seem to affect my sleep, so I'm generally not concerned about having it late in the day.

The consumption slows down over weekends. The morning cup of coffee is always had, but I may only have another one or two cups during the day, as opposed to the three or four on weekdays.

I'm not a snob so I will take coffee in any form. At home, I use a pour-over filter cup with filter paper and cheap store-bought coffee grounds. I like a slight chocolatey flavour, so I gravitate towards [Taylor's Rich Italian](https://www.taylorsofharrogate.co.uk/house-blends/rich-italian/ground) and [M&S Italian Style](https://www.marksandspencer.com/food/italian-style-ground-coffee/p/fdp21041938#intid=pid_pg1pip96g6r1c5). At the office, I'm quite content with the instant coffee available ([NESCAFÉ Gold](https://www.nescafe.com/gold) or [NESCAFÉ Azera](https://www.nescafe.com/gb/coffees/range/nescafe-azera)). (I have no opinions on decaf coffee as I have never consumed it).

I don't know what the outcome of this detox will be, except that it will (in fact, it already has) turn into  blog fodder. I would be curious to hear from folks who've already tried this in some shape or form. Do reach out.
