My attempt to turn this website into a disjointed, [mini-social-media-site](/notebook/2026/03/duodecennium/) for myself has resulted in yet another brand new section: [a gig timeline](/gigs/).

I decided not to limit this to just music. I am documenting theatre performances, talks, and other kinds of performance art that I have had the fortune of witnessing.

I have kept meticulous records post September 2022. I wish I had done this all my life, but it's much too late. I will update the pre-2022 section of the timeline as and when I remember, or come across any evidence (e.g., memories from Google Photos) of where I have been.
