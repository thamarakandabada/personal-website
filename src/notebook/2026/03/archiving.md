I spent some time earlier this month archiving two past projects that I had been involved in.

**The first was the podcast—The Bad Take—I used to host with my friend Himal.** We [started in 2018](https://thamara.co.uk/the-bad-take/), and [recorded](https://thamara.co.uk/hit-record/) the occasional episode until our [last one](https://thamara.co.uk/podcast/episode21/) in 2022. Producing regular episodes was no easy task, especially since we both wanted to have conversations of substance. Life got in the way. I moved to a different country. Himal still talks about recording new episodes, but I keep brushing him off. We may sit down again for a chat some day, who knows.

You can access the full archive here: [**thamara.co.uk/podcast**](https://thamara.co.uk/podcast/)

**The second was Debas.** My involvement was minimal; all I did was maintain the website for Rajni and Himal. When it ran out of steam in 2023, the website disappeared, but I had saved everything.

It has all been archived for posterity here: **[thamara.co.uk/debas](https://thamara.co.uk/debas/)**
