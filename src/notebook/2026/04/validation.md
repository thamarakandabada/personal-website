Don't ask me how I found out, but my [Wordle hack](/notebook/2022/01/a-wordle-hack/) has now been cited in two peer-reviewed computer science papers.

I knew about one. This paper by my friend [Nisansa](https://x.com/NisansaDdS) was published in Feb 2022, in the height of the Wordle craze: [Selecting Seed Words for Wordle using Character Statistics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.03457).

I only just found out about this one: [Finding Wordle Strategies That Can be Mastered by Humans](https://drpress.org/ojs/index.php/HSET/article/view/6633).

Not bad for something my tiny brain came up with.
